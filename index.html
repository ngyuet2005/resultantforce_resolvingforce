合力 (resultant force)：一個力，它單獨作用的效果與幾枝力同時作用的總效果相同。力是矢量，要同時考慮大小和方向。 Resultant force: a single force that has the same effect as several forces acting together. Forces are vectors, so both magnitude and direction matter.

\[\vec R=\vec F_1+\vec F_2+\cdots+\vec F_n\]

拆力 (resolving a force)：把一個力拆成互相垂直的兩個分力(分量)，通常是水平和垂直。物體靜止 ⇒ 合力為零 ⇒ 水平、垂直方向各自平衡。 Resolving a force: split one force into two perpendicular components, usually horizontal and vertical. At rest ⇒ resultant is zero ⇒ forces balance separately horizontally and vertically.

\[F_x=F\cos\theta,\qquad F_y=F\sin\theta\qquad(\theta\text{ 由水平量度 / measured from the horizontal})\]

口訣：角 θ 的鄰邊用 \(\cos\theta\)，對邊用 \(\sin\theta\)。θ 若由垂直線量度，兩者互換！ Rule: the side adjacent to θ uses \(\cos\theta\); the side opposite uses \(\sin\theta\). If θ is measured from the vertical, they swap!

斜面問題：取兩條軸 —— 沿斜面和垂直斜面。重量 \(W\) 向下，要拆成這兩個方向。 Incline problems: choose two axes — along the incline and perpendicular to it. Weight \(W\) points down and must be resolved along these axes.

關鍵：斜面傾角 \(\theta\) 剛好等於 \(W\) 與「斜面法線」的夾角。所以 垂直斜面的分量是 θ 的鄰邊 → \(W\cos\theta\)；沿斜面的分量是對邊 → \(W\sin\theta\)。 Key: the incline angle \(\theta\) equals the angle between \(W\) and the normal to the surface. So the perpendicular component is adjacent → \(W\cos\theta\); the component along the slope is opposite → \(W\sin\theta\).
